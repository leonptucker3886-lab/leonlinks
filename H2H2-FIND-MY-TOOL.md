# Head-to-Head II: Find My Tool (datasheet)

Branch: `datasheet/h2h2-find-my-tool` (never merged to main)

## What it does
Adds a "Find My Tool" section to the LeonLink home page, with a nav link.
Type what you need (e.g. "share links", "fix grammar", "see the code") and it points you to:
- **Linkline**: share and edit your links
- **ClearCopy**: grammar and copy cleanup
- **GitHub source**: the code behind LeonLink

Three quick-pick buttons give one-tap answers.

## Pitch
Visitors shouldn't have to guess which LeonLink tool they need. Find My Tool turns the home page into a one-question guide: say what you want, get sent to the right tool.

## Known limits
- Not yet browser-tested or checked on a Vercel preview.
- ClearCopy link (clearcopy.leonlink.net) may not open until DNS is set up.
